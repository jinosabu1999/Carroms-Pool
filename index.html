THE QUICK GUIDE · 01

Learn the angles.

Simple controls up front. The deeper rules when you need them.

Aiming & Striking

Place your striker with the compact baseline control, then pull back directly from the striker and release. Drag farther for a stronger shot; the board shows your live direction guide.

Keyboard controls

Focus the board: Left / Right moves the striker, A / D adjusts direction, W / S fine-tunes strength, and Space or Enter takes the shot. Press C to center the striker or F for Board Focus.

Classic Mode

Pocket your assigned pieces: Player 1 / You = White, Player 2 / AI = Black. Black carrom men are the opponent's pieces in Classic, so pocketing them gives the opponent points. The Red Queen gives +3 points but must be covered by pocketing one of your own discs immediately on your next shot!

Disc Pool Mode

No Queen! Just race to clear all your assigned carrom men before your opponent clears theirs.

Time Attack Blitz

Solo 60-second arcade rush. Each disc pocketed awards +5 seconds (+10s for Queen) and builds a consecutive Combo Score Multiplier!

Practice Court

A relaxed solo board for learning touch, angle and power. It has no timer, match rewards or career-stat impact. Use Reset board whenever you want a fresh rack.

Striker Foul Penalty

If your striker falls into a pocket, it is a foul! You lose your turn and 1 of your previously pocketed pieces is returned to the center of the board.
